So I have been watching and playing things, first up: Star Trek Lower Decks:
For what its worth its a really fun and good star trek show. I didnt watch it before because it was sold to me as the amercan adult animated comedy of star trek, and it certainly looks like it. But getting more into it, it revealed that it really is a star trek show at heart and the animation is actually far better than I expected. I do still think that the comedy is the main part holding it back, it relis too much on referential humor that becomes less and less specific towards the later seasons. I think it could have been fine just sticking to more obscoure references and jokes and otherwise scatological humor instead of "CAPTAIN JANEWAY !!!! VOYAGER !!!! NICK LARCANO !!!!", anyway. I suggest you give it a watch, its good.

Another thing is Go-Go Town, which sold itself as a more management heavy animalcrossing.
And I gotta say, its not that. The villagers barely matter an its more cozy-chungus factorio than anything else. I do enjoy a good factory management game so I was not negatively taken aback by tat but the animal crossing elements it has attached to the production management aspect of the ame felt undercooked.
The fishing is the most basic it can be, waiting and pressing a button. The fossils are the same, you find them and place them in your town hall but its so easy to find them as to where it feels like you are just doing a gacha thing. The Villagers try to do the Animal Crossing thing where they try to make you care about unique ones that you would want to collect. But since you are mainly just putting them in a stall at a shop you never ever see them anyway and also you cant really interact with them at all, so there is no personality there.
I really like the vehicle mechanics in it, you can find different ones which you can load up with loot, its nice.
Anyway, by the end of the game I saw that they used AI assets so it gets a FAT 0/10 from me, womp womp. Opinel Schmopinel
